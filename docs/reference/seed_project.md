WokTuna is an installable PWA that helps tune a handpan (a dished metal percussion instrument) by showing WHERE on each oval tone field to hammer and along WHICH AXIS. Each tone field resonates simultaneously at 3 partials: fundamental (center/dimple), octave (long axis), compound fifth (short axis). Hammering near center raises pitch, near the edge lowers it. No existing tuner app (Linotune, TonalEnergy, Spectroid) shows this spatial guidance -- that's the product gap. Domain-knowledge source: handpan-stimm-app-recherche.md at repo root (German). Full architecture/build plan: ~/.claude/plans/rippling-hugging-raven.md (local, not in repo).
