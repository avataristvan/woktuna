Chunk 5.5 (review-driven fix pass on Chunks 2-4) complete, committed to master as 5304ad2. Fixed all items from review_findings_chunks_2_4: (1) autocorrelation.ts now correlates over a CORRELATION_WINDOW_SAMPLES=4096 leading window independent of the full AnalyserNode frame length, cutting measured per-call time from ~158ms to ~5.7ms on a 32768-sample/48kHz frame (verified with a throwaway tsx timing script, not committed) -- well under the 16.7ms rAF budget. Also cached cssWidth/cssHeight from the resize handler instead of calling getBoundingClientRect() every draw() frame.
